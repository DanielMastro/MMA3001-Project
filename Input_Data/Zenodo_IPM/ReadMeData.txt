Data Psi_d.csv; Psi_q.csv; Torque.csv: 
dq-Flux and Torque for one electrical rotation over 2.5 seconds for 2331 APs: 
Im (=Iphase) in 10A steps from 0 to 620A 
Gamma in 10 degree steps from -180 to 180 degrees. Gamma is defined in GammaDefinition.pdf

In Psidq__Torque.xlsx all the operating points are listed also Gamma/Im and resulting Id/Iq in the same order as the dataset. 

The tables from the csv file were saved as tables and as matrices in the .mat file. Since the original data contains duplicate data points for I=0A at different angles, the matrices are also saved without these duplicates. 

Other data regarding the motor is listed in the accompanying paper. If the maps and motor data are used, please cite the associated paper.
